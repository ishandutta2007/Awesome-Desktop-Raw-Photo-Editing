# Top Desktop RAW Photo Editing
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Professional RAW Photo Editing, Non-Destructive Workflows, Color Management & Desktop Photo Development*

**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial desktop applications** and **open-source projects** for **Desktop RAW Photo Editing**. These tools enable photographers to process RAW files from digital cameras with advanced controls for exposure, color, detail, and non-destructive editing workflows.

**Examples** include Adobe Lightroom Classic, Capture One Pro, DxO PhotoLab, ON1 Photo RAW, Exposure X7, Skylum Luminar Neo, darktable, RawTherapee, Apple Photos, and ACDSee Photo Studio Professional (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: RAW photo editing has excellent mature open-source options. **darktable**, **RawTherapee**, **ART**, **digiKam**, and related projects deliver professional-grade non-destructive editing, advanced color science, and full local control. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Adobe Lightroom Classic](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom-classic.html)**  
  Industry-standard desktop RAW editor and cataloging application with powerful non-destructive editing, AI-powered tools, and deep integration with Photoshop.

- **[Capture One Pro](https://www.captureone.com/)**  
  High-end professional RAW editor known for exceptional color rendering, tethering support, and advanced layer-based editing favored by studio and commercial photographers.

- **[DxO PhotoLab](https://www.dxo.com/dxo-photolab/)**  
  Advanced RAW converter featuring DxO’s optical corrections, DeepPRIME noise reduction, and precise color and detail controls.

- **[ON1 Photo RAW](https://www.on1.com/products/photo-raw/)**  
  All-in-one RAW editor and photo organizer with AI tools, layers, effects, and a modern non-destructive workflow.

- **[Exposure X7](https://exposure.software/)**  
  Creative RAW photo editor focused on film-inspired looks, fast browsing, and non-destructive editing with strong creative controls.

- **[Skylum Luminar Neo](https://skylum.com/luminar)**  
  AI-powered photo editor with advanced tools for sky replacement, relighting, structure, and creative enhancements on RAW files.

- **[Apple Photos](https://www.apple.com/macos/photos/)**  
  Built-in macOS photo application with solid RAW support, machine learning enhancements, and seamless integration into the Apple ecosystem.

- **[ACDSee Photo Studio Professional](https://www.acdsee.com/)**  
  Feature-rich photo editor and digital asset manager with robust RAW processing, layers, and organization tools.

- **[Affinity Photo 2](https://affinity.serif.com/photo/)**  
  Professional photo editing application with strong RAW development capabilities, layers, and a one-time purchase model.

- **[Corel AfterShot Pro](https://www.coreldraw.com/en/product/aftershot-pro/)**  
  Fast RAW photo workflow tool focused on high-volume editing, batch processing, and efficient catalog management.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[darktable](https://github.com/darktable-org/darktable)**  
  Powerful open-source photography workflow application and RAW developer. Features non-destructive editing, advanced color management, scene-referred workflow, and extensive modules for professional use.

- **[RawTherapee](https://github.com/Beep558/RawTherapee)**  
  Advanced open-source RAW image processing program with excellent demosaicing, detailed exposure and color controls, and a highly configurable interface.

- **[ART (Another RawTherapee)](https://github.com/artpixls/ART)**  
  Fork of RawTherapee focused on improved usability, modern features, and continued development of a high-quality open-source RAW editor.

- **[digiKam](https://github.com/KDE/digikam)**  
  Comprehensive open-source digital photo management application with strong RAW support, advanced editing tools, face recognition, and extensive metadata handling.

- **[PhotoFlow](https://github.com/aferrero2707/PhotoFlow)**  
  Non-destructive open-source photographic editor with a node-based layer system and good RAW processing capabilities.

- **[Filmulator](https://github.com/CarVac/filmulator-gui)**  
  Open-source RAW editor inspired by film processing, offering a unique tone-mapping approach and simplified yet powerful workflow.

- **[LibRaw](https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw)**  
  Foundational open-source library for reading and processing RAW files from a wide range of digital cameras. Used by many editors.

- **[Rawspeed](https://github.com/darktable-org/rawspeed)**  
  Fast open-source RAW decoding library used by darktable and other projects for high-performance camera RAW support.

- **[GIMP + RAW plugins](https://www.gimp.org/)**  
  While primarily a raster editor, GIMP combined with plugins and external RAW processors provides a capable open-source photo editing pipeline.

- **[vkdt](https://github.com/jo/vkdt)**  
  Experimental open-source GPU-accelerated RAW processing pipeline focused on high performance and modern graphics APIs.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Primary professional RAW editors: **darktable**, **RawTherapee**, **ART**.
- Photo management + editing: **digiKam**.
- Specialized / alternative approaches: **Filmulator**, **PhotoFlow**, **vkdt**.
- Core libraries: **LibRaw**, **Rawspeed**.
- Accepting that commercial applications (Lightroom Classic, Capture One, DxO PhotoLab, ON1, Luminar) still lead in polished UI, AI features, ecosystem integrations, and out-of-the-box convenience for many photographers.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
Use **darktable** or **RawTherapee/ART** as the core RAW developer → manage libraries with **digiKam** → extend with **LibRaw** for custom tools. Ideal for photographers and teams who want full control over their editing pipeline, no subscription fees, and the ability to run entirely offline.
